Card Arrangement Options for Process Power-Up

Review the proposed visual layouts below to choose the best look for your cards view.

Option 1: Modern 2-Column Card Grid (Visual Card Tiles)

Cards are laid out in a responsive 2-column grid of glassmorphic tiles with progress bars, status tags, list badges, and direct action buttons.

Option 2: Linear / Jira-Style High-Density Table View

Cards are laid out in structured rows with clear column headers (Card Name, List, Segmented Progress, Status Pill, Assignee, Actions).

Option 3: List Dropdown Focus View

Focuses directly on cards from the chosen list with clean expanded rows.
